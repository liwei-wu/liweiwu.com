Liwei Wu (吴立威) is an AI application entrepreneur and the founder of [DeepFlywheel][entity-deepflywheel], where he explores how AI can be used to build a new generation of applications and software.

Previously, Liwei served as Director of R&D at [SenseTime][entity-sensetime] and was one of the company's early founding employees, contributing to and witnessing its journey from startup to public listing. He founded its face liveness detection and industrial vision R&D teams, helping the resulting AI technologies reach hundreds of millions of users and be deployed at scale in applications including smartphones and high-speed rail.

Liwei earned his bachelor's degree from [Nanjing University][entity-nanjingUniversity] and his master's degree from [Tsinghua University][entity-tsinghuaUniversity]. He is currently building his company full-time while pursuing a part-time Doctor of Engineering in Innovation Leadership at Tsinghua University, with a research focus on intelligent hardware-software systems.
